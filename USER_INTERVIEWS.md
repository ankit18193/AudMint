# AudMint User Interview Insights

## Interview 1: CTO of a Series A Startup (35 employees)
- **Problem**: "We have people on Cursor and people on Copilot. It's a mess. I have no idea how much we are actually spending."
- **Insight**: Need a way to quickly identify "Overlap" between coding tools.
- **Audit Rule Created**: Duplicate Coding Tool Detection.

## Interview 2: Operations Manager (15 employees)
- **Problem**: "I hate having to sign up for things just to see if they are useful."
- **Insight**: Audit must be "No-Login-Required" to get maximum engagement.
- **Architecture Change**: Switched to localStorage persistence.

## Interview 3: Freelance Consultant
- **Problem**: "I want to show my clients how much money I saved them."
- **Insight**: Shareable, professional report pages are a must-have.
- **Feature Created**: Public Report URL (/report/[id]).

## Key Takeaways
1. **Speed is King**: Audit should take <60 seconds.
2. **Numbers Must Be Real**: Users don't trust "Estimated" savings; they want specific tiers.
3. **The 'Aha' Moment**: Seeing the "Annual Savings" figure in a large font is the primary conversion driver.
