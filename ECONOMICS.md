# AudMint Unit Economics

## 1. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Primary Source**: Organic (Viral reports, SEO, Twitter).
- **Target CAC**: <$5.00 per qualified lead.
- **Incentive**: Free value (The Audit) is the primary driver.

## 2. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- **Direct Revenue**: $0 (Product is free).
- **Indirect Revenue**: Referral fees and consultation contracts for Credex.
- **Lead Value**: Estimated $50 - $200 per qualified B2B lead based on industry benchmarks.

## 3. Operational Costs (Burn)
- **Hosting**: $20/mo (Vercel + Render).
- **Database**: $0 (Supabase Free Tier).
- **AI API (Claude)**: ~$0.05 per audit. At 1,000 audits/mo = $50.
- **Total Maintenance**: ~$70/mo.

## 4. Break-even Analysis
- If 1 out of 100 leads converts to a $1,000 Credex consultation, the product is highly profitable.
- 1,000 audits -> 200 leads -> 2 conversions = $2,000 revenue vs $70 cost.
