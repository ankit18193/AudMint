# AudMint Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Target Personas
- **The "SaaS-Stressed" CTO**: Managing a growing team with ballooning AI credit bills.
- **The Fractional CFO**: Looking for low-hanging fruit to reduce burn rates for client startups.
- **The Solo-Founder**: Wanting to ensure they aren't paying for "The Same Tool Twice" (e.g., Cursor + Copilot).

## 2. Distribution Channels
- **Product Hunt**: Launching as a "Free Utility for the AI Era."
- **LinkedIn**: Viral shareable reports ("We just found $4k in waste with AudMint").
- **Twitter/X**: Targeted threads on "The Hidden Cost of AI Redundancy."

## 3. The Lead-Gen Loop
1. **Low Friction**: No sign-up required to run the audit.
2. **Immediate Value**: High-fidelity UI showing exact savings within 60 seconds.
3. **The Hook**: "Email me the full 12-month projection" captures the lead.
4. **The Upsell**: Consultation CTA for audits >$500/mo, funneling high-value users to Credex services.

## 4. Key Metrics
- **Completion Rate**: % of users who finish step 3.
- **Lead Conversion**: % of auditors who provide an email.
- **Viral Coefficient**: % of public reports that generate a new audit session.
